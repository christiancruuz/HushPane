FROM THE MAKER

About HushPane

I’m Christian, a software engineer and founder who loves building things that make everyday life a little easier. HushPane started with a simple thought: I wanted tools like these to exist, so I made them and shared them.

If you like what I’ve built here, check out my main brainchild, Uni-ke. It’s where I’m putting that same energy on a much bigger scale.

If HushPane is useful to you, you can buy me a coffee.

Dressed like hardware

  • Frutiger Aero
  • Cybercore
  • Y2K

The control deck looks like a gadget on purpose. Frutiger Aero is the glossy, hopeful half: sky-blue buttons, brushed metal, and the early-2000s idea that a tool could look friendly and still feel exact. Cybercore is the glass over the readout — scanlines, a status light, a screen already mid-task. Y2K is the posture: rivets, all-caps labels, and buttons chunky enough that pressing one feels deliberate.

Why I keep it open

I use HushPane almost every time I pitch. The script sits near the camera and scrolls at a pace I can talk over, so the words stay in the corner of my eye while I stay with the person across the table or on the call. When the conversation turns, I pause it. When I need the next line, it is already waiting.

Share only the window or tab you are presenting and the prompt stays on your side of the screen. That is the whole trick. I get to sound like I know what I built, because I do, and I am not hunting through a notes app to prove it.
