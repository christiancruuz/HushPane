ClearCue

Free · 1.0.0

A script that floats above your screen and stays off the share.

Paste your words, press start, and share only the window or browser tab you are presenting. ClearCue stays on your side of the call.

Download for Mac macOS 13 or later. Apple silicon and Intel. 229 MB. Download for Windows Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. 107 MB.

On a Mac, open the DMG and drag ClearCue to Applications. On Windows, run the installer. If SmartScreen appears, choose More info, then Run anyway.
